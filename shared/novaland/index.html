Novaland

Working project shared for discussion by Philipp Chapkovski.

Working project · shared for discussion · updated August 2026

Novaland is a virtual-state research environment developed within POLITSOLID at the University of Duisburg-Essen. It creates a controlled institutional setting in which participants experience public services, administration, and collective decisions rather than responding only to abstract descriptions.

Research question

How do experiences with institutions shape political solidarity, trust, and preferences about collective provision? The virtual-state format makes institutional contact observable while retaining experimental control.

Participant journey

01Enter a shared state

Participants encounter a common political and administrative environment.

02Experience institutions

Public services and administrative encounters make state performance concrete.

03Make consequential choices

Participants respond within the environment rather than only reporting general attitudes.

04Measure political responses

The design links institutional experience to solidarity, trust, and collective preferences.

Research contribution

The environment connects behavioural experiments, public administration, and political science. It provides reusable infrastructure for studying how citizens interpret and respond to the state under controlled conditions.
