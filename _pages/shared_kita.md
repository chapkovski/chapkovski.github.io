<div class="quiet-project">
  <p class="quiet-project__status">Working project · shared for discussion · updated August 2026</p>

  <p class="quiet-project__lede">This POLITSOLID project asks whether information and empathy affect participation and solidarity in municipal kindergarten financing. The setting makes redistribution concrete: families use a locally provided service whose costs are shared between parents and the wider public.</p>

  <section aria-labelledby="kita-question">
    <h2 id="kita-question">Research question</h2>
    <p>Can short communications about co-beneficiaries or public financing change willingness to enter a study, judgments about fair parental contributions, and solidarity-related behaviour?</p>
  </section>

  <section aria-labelledby="kita-design">
    <h2 id="kita-design">Linked field and survey experiments</h2>
    <div class="quiet-project__flow quiet-project__flow--two-stage" aria-label="Kindergarten financing research design">
      <div><span>Study 1</span><strong>Invitation-letter field experiment</strong><p>Households receive differently framed invitations; the design observes entry into the survey.</p></div>
      <div><span>Study 2</span><strong>Survey information experiment</strong><p>Participants encounter bounded information or empathy-based communication before reporting fairness preferences and making a solidarity-related decision.</p></div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="quiet-project__callout" aria-labelledby="kita-purpose">
    <h2 id="kita-purpose">Why the design matters</h2>
    <p>The project connects policy feedback, beneficiary visibility, information provision, and behavioural measures in a real municipal service context. It also separates effects on participation from effects among people who have already entered the survey.</p>
  </section>

  <p class="quiet-project__boundary">The manuscript remains a first draft. This page therefore publishes no draft PDF, numerical results, invitation text, treatment wording, internal comments, raw materials, or municipal data.</p>
