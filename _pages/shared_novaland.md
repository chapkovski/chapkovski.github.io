<div class="quiet-project">
  <p class="quiet-project__status">Working project · shared for discussion · updated August 2026</p>

  <p class="quiet-project__lede">Novaland is a virtual-state research environment developed within POLITSOLID at the University of Duisburg-Essen. It creates a controlled institutional setting in which participants experience public services, administration, and collective decisions rather than responding only to abstract descriptions.</p>

  <section aria-labelledby="novaland-question">
    <h2 id="novaland-question">Research question</h2>
    <p>How do experiences with institutions shape political solidarity, trust, and preferences about collective provision? The virtual-state format makes institutional contact observable while retaining experimental control.</p>
  </section>

  <section aria-labelledby="novaland-journey">
    <h2 id="novaland-journey">Participant journey</h2>
    <div class="quiet-project__flow" aria-label="Novaland participant journey">
      <div><span>01</span><strong>Enter a shared state</strong><p>Participants encounter a common political and administrative environment.</p></div>
      <div><span>02</span><strong>Experience institutions</strong><p>Public services and administrative encounters make state performance concrete.</p></div>
      <div><span>03</span><strong>Make consequential choices</strong><p>Participants respond within the environment rather than only reporting general attitudes.</p></div>
      <div><span>04</span><strong>Measure political responses</strong><p>The design links institutional experience to solidarity, trust, and collective preferences.</p></div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="quiet-project__callout" aria-labelledby="novaland-contribution">
    <h2 id="novaland-contribution">Research contribution</h2>
    <p>The environment connects behavioural experiments, public administration, and political science. It provides reusable infrastructure for studying how citizens interpret and respond to the state under controlled conditions.</p>
  </section>

  <p class="quiet-project__boundary">This page contains no live demo, treatment materials, questionnaire wording, participant data, or results.</p>
