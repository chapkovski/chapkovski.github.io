I take on a limited amount of external methods work for research teams, public-sector organisations, and evidence-focused companies. I help turn a research question, draft instrument, or difficult pilot into a design that can be implemented and tested reliably.

## Experiment design audit

**From €1,250 · typically 5–7 business days**

A focused review for a survey or behavioural study that needs a rigorous second look before launch or after a difficult pilot.

The audit can cover:

- the research question, estimand, and identification strategy;
- treatment conditions, randomisation, and implementation logic;
- outcome measurement, survey flow, and respondent burden;
- power, sample planning, and analysis readiness;
- demand effects, attrition, exclusions, and other likely failure modes.

The usual handoff includes an annotated instrument or protocol, a prioritised correction memo, one consolidated revision round, and a call to discuss the recommendations.

## Survey or experiment build

**From €2,400 · typically 8–10 business days**

Implementation support for teams that have an agreed research objective but need a reliable working study.

Depending on the project, this can include:

- translating the design into Qualtrics, oTree, SurveyJS, R, or Python;
- implementing randomisation, branching, validation, and treatment logic;
- preparing test cases, a data schema, and quality-assurance checks;
- documenting the implementation and handing it over to the research team.

The starting price assumes one defined study or instrument. Modest changes around the agreed outcome are included; a new research question, platform, instrument, or substantial redesign is quoted separately.

5. an indicative budget range.
