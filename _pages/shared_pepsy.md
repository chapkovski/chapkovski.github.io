  <p class="quiet-project__status">Working project · shared for discussion · updated August 2026</p>

  <p class="quiet-project__lede">PEPSY studies how citizens understand political solidarity, public services, institutions, and redistribution across European contexts. The first wave combines harmonised measures with nationally grounded survey implementation in seven countries and more than 8,000 completed interviews.</p>

  <section aria-labelledby="pepsy-design">
    <h2 id="pepsy-design">Comparative design</h2>
    <div class="quiet-project__facts">
      <div><strong>Coverage</strong><span>Seven European countries</span></div>
      <div><strong>Scale</strong><span>More than 8,000 participants</span></div>
      <div><strong>Methods</strong><span>Comparative surveys and embedded experimental components</span></div>
      <div><strong>Context</strong><span>POLITSOLID at the University of Duisburg-Essen</span></div>
    </div>
    <p>The design treats cross-country comparison as a measurement problem as well as a substantive one: instruments must retain a common conceptual core while remaining intelligible in different institutional settings.</p>
  </section>

  <section aria-labelledby="pepsy-wave-two">
    <p class="quiet-project__eyebrow">Wave 2 continuation</p>
    <h2 id="pepsy-wave-two">Baltic Crisis module</h2>
    <p>The Wave 2 module extends the comparative programme into a crisis setting. It is designed as a connected spin-off: a shared measurement framework is carried into a new scenario so that information, institutional interpretation, and solidarity can be studied under heightened uncertainty.</p>
    <div class="quiet-project__flow" aria-label="Baltic Crisis module flow">
      <div><span>01</span><strong>Comparative baseline</strong><p>Start from harmonised measures developed for the first survey wave.</p></div>
      <div><span>02</span><strong>Crisis scenario</strong><p>Introduce a bounded, policy-relevant situation without publishing the instrument.</p></div>
      <div><span>03</span><strong>Institutional interpretation</strong><p>Measure how citizens understand responsibility, information, and collective response.</p></div>
      <div><span>04</span><strong>Solidarity outcomes</strong><p>Connect the continuation to the wider comparative research architecture.</p></div>
    </div>
