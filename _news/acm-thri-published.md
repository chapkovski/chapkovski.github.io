Our paper [Teaming Up with Artificial Agents in Non-routine Analytical Tasks](https://doi.org/10.1145/3816428) was published in *ACM Transactions on Human-Robot Interaction*. We study how artificial-agent embodiment shapes team performance and conversational dynamics in a real-life escape-room task.
