On November 18, 2024, I submitted a paper co-authored with [Alexei Zakharov](https://www.alexeizakharov.net/) to a journal (an older version of this paper is available on SSRN).

Our study explores the discrimination of anti-war Russians by pro-war Russians, providing experimental evidence of how political polarization influences interpersonal interactions and decision-making.
