On November 18, 2024, I submitted a paper co-authored with [Alexei Zakharov](https://www.alexeizakharov.net/) to a journal (the old version of this paper iavailable at the [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4355956)). 

Our study explores the discrimination of anti-war Russians by pro-war Russians, providing experimental evidence of how political polarization influences interpersonal interactions and decision-making.
