My paper [Can corruption indices lead to statistical discrimination? Experimental evidence from a within-country setting](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11127-025-01367-1) was published in *Public Choice*. The experiment shows how regional corruption information can shape beliefs about honesty and trustworthiness, widening perception gaps between groups.
