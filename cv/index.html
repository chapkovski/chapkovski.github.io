Profile

  • Postdoctoral researcher in behavioural economics and finance, developing computational experimentation for public policy, financial technology, and AI systems.
  • Expertise in experimental economics, online and laboratory experiments, trading platforms, causal inference, and computational social science.
  • Research focuses on financial technology, information avoidance, political polarisation, cooperation, solidarity, and behavioural responses to digital and institutional environments.
