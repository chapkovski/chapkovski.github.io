<section class="project-showcase__split-section" aria-labelledby="wedr-design">
  <div>
    <p class="project-showcase__eyebrow">Design</p>
    <h2 id="wedr-design">Cooperation when neither partner has the full answer</h2>
    <p>Paired participants work on a joint real-effort task in which each person sees only part of the information needed to succeed. The design can vary whether political disagreement is revealed, concealed, requested, or blocked, while the platform records communication and performance.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="project-showcase__flow" aria-label="Workplace experiment flow">
    <div><span>01</span><strong>Partner assignment</strong><p>Participants are matched for a synchronous collaboration task.</p></div>
    <div><span>02</span><strong>Divided information</strong><p>Each partner receives a different part of the material needed to solve the task.</p></div>
    <div><span>03</span><strong>Communication</strong><p>The interface captures information sharing, timing, and coordination.</p></div>
    <div><span>04</span><strong>Joint performance</strong><p>Outcomes connect communication choices to accuracy and team efficiency.</p></div>
  </div>
</section>
