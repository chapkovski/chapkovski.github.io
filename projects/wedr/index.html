Research question

What happens when political disagreement enters teams?

The project asks how awareness of political distance changes communication and cooperation when people must work with a partner. A real-time task makes information exchange and joint performance directly observable.

Status
Pilot and partial research programme
Design

My role

Design

Cooperation when neither partner has the full answer

Paired participants work on a joint real-effort task in which each person sees only part of the information needed to succeed. The design can vary whether political disagreement is revealed, concealed, requested, or blocked, while the platform records communication and performance.

01Partner assignment

Participants are matched for a synchronous collaboration task.

02Divided information

Each partner receives a different part of the material needed to solve the task.

03Communication

The interface captures information sharing, timing, and coordination.

04Joint performance

Outcomes connect communication choices to accuracy and team efficiency.
